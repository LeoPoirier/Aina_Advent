HOW TO WRITE QUESTIONS FOR THE DAY 5 QUIZ
==========================================

Two question types: multiple choice (MC) and free text (TEXT).
Write them below using the exact format shown — one block per question,
blank line between blocks. I'll transcribe these into the page once
you're done; you don't need to touch any code.

MULTIPLE CHOICE format:
  TYPE: MC
  Q: <the question>
  A: <option 1>
  A: <option 2>
  A: <option 3>
  A: <option 4>          (3 or 4 options is fine, doesn't have to be 4)
  CORRECT: <the exact text of the correct option, copied from above>

FREE TEXT format:
  TYPE: TEXT
  Q: <the question>
  ACCEPTED: <answer 1>, <answer 2>, <answer 3>   (any of these count as correct,
                                                   matching is case-insensitive
                                                   and just needs to appear
                                                   somewhere in her answer)
